XMO Mini · Concept in validation

Portfolio reporting for a one- or two-person PMO

A small project management office can spend much of its review cycle chasing updates. Mini explores a lighter way to collect evidence, flag exceptions and prepare decisions.

Availability: Discuss fit and scope. An enquiry does not reserve a slot or place an order. Any pilot requires qualification, confirmed capacity, agreed scope and terms, and separate approval.

Discuss Mini pilot fit

Where Mini may fit

You have a PMO and a live portfolio, but updates arrive in different formats and issues are escalated late. Start with one review cycle and a named owner for each source.

What a scoped pilot could cover

Map the existing status report, milestone and dependency records, and escalation thresholds. Agree a short update format and assemble an example exception list with linked sources, owners and decisions needed.

Your team reviews the evidence and owns the decisions. Software assistance depends on source quality and separately confirmed tools and access. Scope, timing and terms are agreed for each pilot.

Compare the pathways

Starter explores coordination before a formal PMO exists. Decision Cycle focuses on one executive portfolio forum. The Change Impact Agent is a separate research prototype in development.
