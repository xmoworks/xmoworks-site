XMO Starter · Concept in validation

Lightweight project governance for growing teams

When work crosses teams, informal updates can hide priorities, owners and dependencies. Starter explores how a growing organization can make those commitments visible.

Availability: Enquiries are open. An enquiry does not reserve a slot or place an order. Any pilot requires qualification, confirmed capacity, agreed scope and terms, and separate approval.

Discuss Starter pilot fit

Who it is for

Growing organizations typically coordinating 3–12 active initiatives without a formal project management office (PMO). A sponsor who can set priorities and an owner who can keep the project list current are important.

Ask separately if you need outsourced project delivery, enterprise redesign or a ready-to-install platform.

What a scoped pilot could cover

Start with an approved project list, owners, dates, recent updates and examples of decisions that stalled. Agree how to flag exceptions, who resolves them and when to escalate.

A possible outcome is a shared list of priorities and owners, a short review agenda and an action log. Use existing tools where they fit. Your team retains responsibility for delivery and decisions.

Choose the right level of support

A task tracker records assignments. A coordination routine also defines who can change priorities and where dependencies get resolved. If you already run a small PMO, explore XMO Mini. Read when a formal PMO may help.
