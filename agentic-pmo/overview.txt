First workflow · Change Impact

## Before you accept a change, understand what it affects.

Project managers and PMO teams often need to decide whether to fund, swap or defer a proposed scope change. The Change Impact Agent is a prototype in development, tested internally on fictional cases. It compares a proposed change with the agreed project baseline and uses supplied estimates and delivery information to draft a brief with options and gaps in the evidence.

Cost and schedule implications depend on the information provided. Unsupported figures and dates remain unknown. The responsible decision owner reviews the brief and decides. The survey is for research. You can separately opt in to development updates. Please do not submit confidential project information. For a recurring executive review, see [Decision Cycle](https://xmoworks.ae/decision-cycle/). Read [how to check an AI-assisted portfolio brief](https://xmoworks.ae/insights/article.html?slug=checking-ai-portfolio-brief).

### Check the baseline

Understand what was agreed and what the new request changes.

### Surface the gaps

Ask for missing estimates and identify conflicting evidence.

### Compare the choices

Examine supported implications of funding, swapping or deferring scope.

### Prepare the decision

Put assumptions and trade-offs in front of the person who decides.
