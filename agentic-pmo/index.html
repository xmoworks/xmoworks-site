First workflow · Change Impact

Before you accept a change, understand what it affects.

The Change Impact Agent is being developed to connect the request to the baseline, surface missing estimates, compare supported options and prepare a decision brief. It will identify uncertainty rather than invent a cost or delivery date.

Check the baseline

Understand what was agreed and what the new request changes.

Surface the gaps

Ask for missing estimates and identify conflicting evidence.

Compare the choices

Examine supported implications of funding, swapping or deferring scope.

Prepare the decision

Put assumptions and trade-offs in front of the person who decides.
