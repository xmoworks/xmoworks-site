XMO Decision Cycle for portfolio governance teams

When decision preparation slows the governance cycle

PMO and transformation teams often bring project status, resource constraints and unresolved decisions together from several sources. Decision Cycle is designed for teams with an executive sponsor and a recurring portfolio decision forum.

What a design-partner pilot would cover

Agree the forum, decision responsibilities and source access. Use approved project reports, risks and dependencies to prepare a source-linked executive brief that makes decisions, owners, options and open assumptions visible. The proposed pilot includes a capacity and exception view and a decision and action log. XMO would work with your team to agree what to measure before the pilot. Your team reviews the evidence and retains responsibility for decisions and approvals.
