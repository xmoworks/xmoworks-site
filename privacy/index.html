Privacy and data use

Speritas Digital LLC operates XMO Works.

This is a plain-language product-stage privacy notice and will be reviewed as XMO Works expands.

Information and choices

When you submit XMO Finder, Early Access or a contact form, XMO Works stores the information you provide so we can deliver the requested diagnostic, respond to you, improve our products and understand recurring project-management problems.

Where you explicitly consent to marketing, we may use your contact details to send relevant XMO Works updates and resources. Diagnostic-contact consent and marketing consent are treated separately.

Optional analytics and advertising technologies remain off until you choose. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics and XMO Works first-party usage measurement help us understand site performance. If you accept advertising, the LinkedIn Insight Tag records campaign activity and Finder-completion conversions. You can change these choices at any time using the Privacy choices control.

Campaign parameters included in a link may be carried into a form submission so we can attribute a requested diagnostic to the relevant campaign.

XMO Works does not sell submitted personal information to advertisers. We may use service providers that process information on our behalf for hosting, analytics, AI-assisted processing, email delivery and workflow automation.

AI-generated diagnostic or content outputs are reviewed or controlled according to the workflow in use. Do not submit confidential information that you are not authorized to share.

For questions, access or deletion requests, contact hello@xmoworks.ae.
